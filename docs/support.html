FAQ

How do I play? Swipe in any direction. The ball slides until it hits a wall and paints every tile it passes. Paint the whole floor to clear the level.

I'm stuck. Tap Undo, Restart, or the lightbulb for a hint. A hint shows your next winning move, or tells you if the board can no longer be finished.

How do I change the theme? Open Settings and pick Midnight, Ember Dusk or Mint Grove.

Why are there ads? SlideBloom is free and supported by ads. You get free hints first; after that a short optional video unlocks another. You can change ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
