SlideBloom

Swipe. Slide. Bloom. A calm sliding-paint puzzle for iPhone and iPad: the chrome ball glides until it stops, painting every tile it crosses. Fill the whole board to make it bloom.

Features

  • 150 puzzles in 15 chapters
  • Three calming themes
  • Unlimited undo, hints when you're stuck
  • Plays offline
