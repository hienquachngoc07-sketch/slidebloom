Privacy Policy

SlideBloom by QG · Last updated 3 October 2026

Summary

SlideBloom has no accounts and does not collect personal information itself. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect data as described below.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, best move counts, remaining free hints, the selected theme, and sound and haptics settings are saved locally on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

SlideBloom uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show optional rewarded videos (to unlock hints) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction and usage data
  • Diagnostic data such as crash and performance information

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK), you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Learn more in Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

SlideBloom is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
